# Kai’s criteria for a good website

1. Click-to-call (`tel:` with a number)
2. Homepage lead capture (form, chat, or estimate/quote/contact CTA)
3. Not a one-pager (at least two real internal pages)
4. Service pages
5. Service areas
6. On-site reviews (not only Google)
7. Booking calendar
8. Privacy / terms
9. Gallery of most recent projects uploaded by contractor app (carousel type on home page. clicking on project opens to a dedicated page for that project)
