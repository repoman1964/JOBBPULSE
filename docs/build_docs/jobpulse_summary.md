# JobbPulse Summary

## Overview

JobbPulse is an all-in-one marketing and lead-conversion platform built for visual home-service contractors.

It is designed for businesses such as painters, tree-service companies, stump-removal companies, hardscapers, landscape installers, flooring installers, fencing and deck contractors and roofers. These businesses complete valuable, highly visual work but often fail to consistently market that work, follow up with incoming leads or turn completed projects into long-term business assets.

JobbPulse solves that problem through three connected components:

1. The Contractor App
2. The Smart Website
3. The JobbPulse Directory

## Core Value Proposition

JobbPulse helps visual contractors stop losing leads and turn every completed job into proof that can help win the next one.

A contractor documents work once. JobbPulse then uses that work to create marketing content, strengthen the contractor's website, build local project pages, support review generation and create future demand.

## The Contractor App

The Contractor App is the contractor-facing Job-to-Content Engine.

After completing a project, the contractor:

1. Creates a new job
2. Uploads at least three photos, typically showing the project before and after completion
3. Records a short voice summary describing the work performed
4. Reviews the content generated by JobbPulse
5. Approves or regenerates the content before publication

JobbPulse uses the photos, voice summary and job information to create:

- Social media posts
- Project descriptions
- Website portfolio content
- Local job pages
- Directory content
- Future advertising assets

The workflow is designed to be fast, mobile-first and simple enough for a contractor, crew member or office employee to use without writing marketing copy.

## The Smart Website

The Smart Website is the lead-conversion and customer-communication layer of JobbPulse.

It includes capabilities such as:

- Immediate follow-up after form submissions
- Missed-call text back
- AI-powered SMS conversations
- AI phone answering
- Website chat
- Contact forms and lead capture
- Automated review requests
- Lead nurturing and follow-up
- Appointment and estimate booking
- Ongoing customer communication

The Smart Website also connects directly to the Contractor App. Once a completed job is approved, its photos and project content can automatically appear on the contractor's website through a recent-jobs gallery, feed, carousel or project portfolio.

This creates a living website that continuously updates with real proof of completed work.

## The JobbPulse Directory

The JobbPulse Directory is an owned local-services platform built around contractor portfolios and completed projects.

Each participating contractor can have:

- A contractor profile or portfolio page
- An About Us page
- Service and location information
- A gallery of completed projects
- A dedicated page for each completed job

Each project page can contain:

- Before-and-after photos
- A project description
- Service category
- City or service area
- Contractor information
- Calls to action for homeowners

The directory is owned and controlled by JobbPulse. It is intended to become:

- A discovery platform for homeowners
- A source of local search traffic
- A growing archive of completed work
- A lead-generation channel for contractors
- A proprietary data and distribution asset for JobbPulse

Unlike third-party social platforms, JobbPulse controls the page structure, content and distribution channel.

## How the System Works

The core workflow is:

```text
Create Job
→ Upload 3+ before-and-after photos
→ Record a voice summary
→ Generate content with AI
→ Review and approve
→ Publish to social media, Smart Website and JobbPulse Directory
```

At the same time, the Smart Website responds to incoming leads, recovers missed calls, follows up with prospects and requests reviews from completed customers.

The result is a connected system that captures completed work, publishes it, builds trust and helps convert attention into new business.

## Ideal Customer

JobbPulse is built for visual home-service businesses where one additional booked project can justify the monthly subscription.

The ideal customer:

- Completes visually impressive work
- Has access to before-and-after photos
- Has an average job value of at least roughly $500, ideally $1,000 or more
- Benefits from local reputation and reviews
- Misses calls or responds slowly to leads
- Does not consistently publish completed work
- Wants more visibility without becoming a full-time marketer

## What Makes JobbPulse Different

Many agencies already offer websites, CRM automation, missed-call text back, review requests and chat widgets.

JobbPulse combines those familiar services with two proprietary assets:

- A Contractor App that continuously turns completed jobs into marketing content
- A JobbPulse-owned directory that publishes and organizes that content into contractor and project pages

The Smart Website receives approved job content automatically. The directory turns completed projects into owned local pages. Social publishing extends the same content to the contractor's existing audience.

Together, these components create a system that typical website agencies and general-purpose automation providers do not easily reproduce.

## Strategic Positioning

JobbPulse should be presented as one clear solution rather than three separate products.

A strong positioning statement is:

> JobbPulse helps visual contractors stop losing leads and turn every completed job into proof that wins the next one.

A website-led version is:

> JobbPulse is a lead-converting contractor website that automatically turns completed jobs into fresh proof and marketing content.

## Business Model

JobbPulse is planned as a monthly service priced in the general range of $197 to $297 per contractor.

The economic argument is simple: for the targeted trades, one additional booked job can potentially cover the monthly cost.

The early go-to-market plan includes free pilot users to accelerate product feedback, build completed-job pages and develop real-world proof before scaling paid acquisition.
