# JobbPulse

JobbPulse is an all-in-one marketing and lead conversion platform built specifically for visual home-service contractors.

It is designed for businesses such as painters, tree service companies, hardscapers, landscapers, flooring installers, fencing and deck contractors and roofers. These businesses do valuable, highly visual work but often fail to consistently market it, follow up with leads or turn completed jobs into long-term business assets.

JobbPulse solves that problem through three connected components: the Contractor App, the Smart Website and the JobbPulse Directory.

## The Contractor App

The Contractor App makes it easy for contractors to turn completed jobs into marketing content.

After finishing a project, the contractor uploads before-and-after photos and records a short voice description of the work performed. JobbPulse uses that information to generate professional content such as social media posts, project descriptions and local job pages.

The contractor does not need to write copy, organize photos or manually create posts. They simply document the work they already completed and JobbPulse handles the rest.

## The Smart Website

The Smart Website is more than a standard contractor website.

A traditional website displays services, photos, a phone number and a contact form. A Smart Website actively responds to leads and helps convert them into customers.

It can include:

- Immediate follow-up after a form submission
- Missed-call text back
- AI-powered SMS conversations
- AI phone answering
- Website chat
- Automated review requests
- Lead nurturing and follow-up
- Appointment and estimate booking
- Ongoing customer communication

Instead of allowing a lead to sit unnoticed in an inbox, JobbPulse responds quickly and begins moving the prospect toward the next step.

The Smart Website also connects directly to the Contractor App. Every time the contractor adds a completed job, the new photos and project content can automatically appear on the website in a recent-jobs gallery, scrolling feed or project portfolio.

As the contractor completes more work, the website continually updates itself with fresh, real-world proof.

## The JobbPulse Directory

Each completed job can also be published to the JobbPulse local services directory.

The directory is owned and controlled by JobbPulse. It creates searchable pages showing real work completed by local contractors in specific services and locations.

Over time, the directory becomes:

- A local discovery platform for homeowners
- A source of search engine traffic
- A growing archive of completed work
- A lead generation channel for contractors
- A proprietary data and distribution asset for JobbPulse

Unlike social media platforms, the directory is not controlled by a third party. JobbPulse owns the pages, the content structure and the distribution channel.

## How the System Works

The full JobbPulse workflow is simple:

A contractor completes a job, uploads photos and records a voice summary through the Contractor App.

JobbPulse then generates the marketing content and distributes it across the contractor’s social media accounts, Smart Website and JobbPulse Directory.

At the same time, the Smart Website handles incoming leads, responds to missed calls, follows up with prospects and requests reviews from completed customers.

The result is a connected marketing system that captures the contractor’s work, publishes it, builds trust and converts attention into new business.

## Who JobbPulse Is For

JobbPulse is built for visual home-service businesses where completed jobs provide strong proof of quality and where one additional booked project can easily justify the monthly cost.

The ideal customer:

- Completes visually impressive work
- Has access to before-and-after photos
- Has an average job value of at least several hundred dollars
- Benefits from local reputation and reviews
- Misses calls or responds slowly to leads
- Does not consistently post completed work
- Wants more business without becoming a full-time marketer

JobbPulse will initially focus on painting, tree service, stump removal, hardscaping and landscape installation, followed by flooring, fencing, decks and roofing.
