# JobbPulse USP and offer destinations

**Status:** Canonical offer source of truth  
**Updated:** 2026-08-25

When product docs, build specs, sales copy, or engineering defaults disagree, this file wins for **what a finished job becomes** and **what the paid plan includes**.

The contractor still does the same three things: create a job, add photos, talk for about 30 seconds. JobbPulse generates the package, the contractor approves it, and JobbPulse distributes it.

---

## Positioning

> Finish the job. Show JobbPulse what you did. JobbPulse turns it into marketing — on your pages, your website, the JobbPulse directory, and the local homeowners groups where your next customer already hangs out.

The wedge is still **job-to-content**. Neighborhood-group posting and a seasonal social cadence are now part of that same offer, not a separate social-media product.

The contractor app must stay stupid-simple. Group targeting and evergreen cadence are engine/ops work. Do not add a social calendar, per-group composer, or posting dashboard to the contractor app.

---

## What one approved job produces

Every approved completed-job package publishes to **all** of the following:

| # | Destination | What goes live |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facebook business page | Completed-job post on the contractor’s connected Facebook Page |
| 2 | Instagram | Completed-job post on the contractor’s connected Instagram account |
| 3 | Contractor website — home | Job added to the recent-jobs carousel on the front page |
| 4 | Contractor website — job page | A dedicated project page on the contractor’s site for that job |
| 5 | JobbPulse local directory | A permanent project page on the JobbPulse-owned directory property |
| 6 | Google Business Profile | Completed-job post on the contractor’s Google Business Profile |
| 7 | Local Facebook homeowners / neighborhood groups | Posts to **3–5** groups in the contractor’s service area |

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X, and LinkedIn are **not** in the current paid offer. Do not sell them as included destinations until this file is updated.

---

## Evergreen social cadence (between jobs)

In addition to job packages, JobbPulse schedules regular, varied posts to the **social destinations above**:

- Facebook business page
- Instagram
- Google Business Profile
- The same 3–5 local Facebook homeowners / neighborhood groups

Typical themes: weather, holidays, seasonal reminders, “good week to…” prompts for the contractor’s trade. These posts are **not** job pages. They do **not** create website carousel items, website job pages, or directory pages.

Timing should feel irregular (not a fixed weekday slot) so the account does not look automated.

The contractor does not operate a calendar. They can pause the cadence. Job packages still require human approval before first distribution.

---

## Why this is the offer

Agencies already sell websites, missed-call text-back, and generic social posting.

JobbPulse’s difference is one capture event that:

1. Puts real completed work on the contractor’s own Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile
2. Puts that same work in front of neighbors inside local homeowners groups
3. Keeps the contractor’s website current (home carousel + a page per job)
4. Compounds into an owned JobbPulse directory listing
5. Keeps those social surfaces active between jobs so the feed is not empty for weeks

The owned directory remains a long-term asset JobbPulse controls. Neighborhood groups are **reach**, not a replacement for owned pages. We still build pages we own so the business does not depend entirely on Facebook.

---

## Operating constraints (not exclusions)

Facebook group posting is **in scope**. It is also operationally sensitive:

- Use the contractor’s connected Facebook account, not a JobbPulse-owned spam identity
- Cap at 3–5 groups chosen for the service area during onboarding
- Write group copy for neighbors, not ads (softer CTA than the business-page post)
- Respect group rules; if a group blocks posting, skip it and keep the rest of the package
- Never post exact residential addresses or GPS; city / neighborhood only

Account and group-policy risk is an ops problem to manage. It is not a reason to drop groups from the offer.

---

## What the contractor app must not become

Still out of scope for the contractor-facing app:

- A manual social calendar UI
- Per-destination toggles in the main finish-job flow (one approve publishes the locked set)
- CRM, invoicing, dispatch, or a group-management console

Engine, workers, and ops may schedule evergreen posts and fan out to the locked destinations without exposing that machinery in the app.
